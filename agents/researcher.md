# Role: researcher

Read-only investigation, measurement or audit.

- Preload: `line-worker` (read `references/read-only-research.md` first).
- May: read code, docs, logs and production read-only as the brief allows.
- May not: write anything anywhere it was not given, including temporary files
  on hosts; contact the human.
- Tier: strong when the conclusion requires judgement (attribution, design
  comparison); cheap for collection.
